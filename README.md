The Tale of Africa


Africa is a mineral rich diverse country with plenty of scope to prosper and flourish. But due to several difficulties the country always lags behind in terms of wealth creation and management, more than that, human resource management. In terms of growth, Africa is lagging behind several continents that highlights its miserable state. Due to consistent outbreak of diseases that are dangerous, many people in Africa die young due to coming in contact with such illnesses, certain disease outbreaks like, Ebola and HIV AIDS, have impacted Africa in a very miserable way.
